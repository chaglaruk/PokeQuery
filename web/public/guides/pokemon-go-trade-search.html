Trade candidates

Build a trade list without mixing it with your keepers.

Trade searches are useful for surfacing duplicates and other candidates, but they should remain review lists rather than automatic decisions. PokeQuery’s Trade Fodder workflow builds the text while keeping protected categories explicit.

Review first

PokeQuery never trades, transfers or changes a Pokémon. It creates a search string, shows what it does and leaves the final decision inside Pokémon GO to you.

Suggested workflow

  1. Open Trade Fodder.
  2. Review the query and exclusions.
  3. Copy the string into Pokémon GO.
  4. Check every candidate before making a trade.
