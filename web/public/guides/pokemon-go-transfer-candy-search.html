Transfer-candy prep

Prepare your transfer list before the bonus starts.

When a 2× transfer-candy window is active, rebuilding filters wastes time. PokeQuery’s Candy Prep workflow creates a review list ahead of the event while keeping safety exclusions visible.

Use the event as a deadline, not a rush

Build the list early, review it at your own pace and only make transfer decisions inside Pokémon GO after checking the matches. PokeQuery does not transfer anything for you.

Suggested workflow

  1. Open Candy Prep before the event.
  2. Review the generated string and protected categories.
  3. Copy it into Pokémon GO and inspect the results.
  4. Keep the search ready for the bonus window.
